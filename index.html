Frequently Asked Questions

How long does shipping take?
Shipping takes 2-5 business days depending on your city. Cairo and Giza usually receive orders within 2 days, while other governorates may take up to 5 days.
What is your return policy?
You can return any item within 14 days of delivery as long as it's in original condition with tags attached. Contact us via WhatsApp to initiate a return.
Do you ship outside Egypt?
Currently we only ship within Egypt. International shipping is coming soon!
How do I know my size?
Check our size guide page for detailed measurements. If you're unsure, contact us on WhatsApp and we'll help you choose the right size.
Can I change or cancel my order?
Yes, you can change or cancel your order within 2 hours of placing it by contacting us on WhatsApp.
What payment methods do you accept?
We accept Cash on Delivery and WhatsApp-based ordering. Online payment with cards is coming soon.

Shipping & Returns

🚚 Shipping Information

We offer fast and reliable shipping across Egypt. Orders are processed within 24 hours and shipped the next business day.

Shipping Costs:
• Cairo & Giza: 50 EGP
• Alexandria: 60 EGP
• Other Governorates: 70 EGP
• Free shipping on orders over 2000 EGP

📦 Return Policy

We want you to love your purchase. If you're not satisfied, you can return any item within 14 days of delivery.

Conditions:
• Item must be in original condition
• Tags must be attached
• Original packaging required
• Return shipping is customer's responsibility

💱 Exchange Policy

We offer free exchanges within 7 days of delivery if the item is defective or the wrong size was sent.
